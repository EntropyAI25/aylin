# India consumer-protection and product-labelling law for a D2C fashion/apparel e-commerce site (as of Oct 2026)

Research basis: web searches and fetches on 21 Oct 2026. Mostly law-firm and news summaries; primary gazette text was not retrievable. Verify exact rule numbers against the gazette before relying on them. Items marked [UNVERIFIED] come from background knowledge or a single weak source.

## 1. Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020 and the 2026 amendment

### Takeaway
A D2C brand with its own site and own stock is an "inventory e-commerce entity". It must show legal identity, a grievance officer, full price breakup, return/refund/warranty terms and country of origin, and must not use unfair practices or fake reviews. The Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) (Amendment) Rules, 2026 take effect 1 January 2027. They add a yearly dark-pattern self-audit with a displayed certificate, a "lowest price in last 30 days" rule for price-cut claims, and National Consumer Helpline (NCH) participation.

### Cited Findings
**Amendment status (flag: first major change since 2020)**
- The Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) (Amendment) Rules, 2026 were reported on 11-15 Sept 2026 (PIB release; gazette reference reported as G.S.R. 789(E), not verified). They take effect 1 January 2027. — [LiveLaw](https://www.livelaw.in/consumer-cases/government-amends-e-commerce-rules-to-strengthen-consumer-protection-550184); [S.S. Rana](https://ssrana.in/articles/consumer-protection-e-commerce-amendment-rules-2026-recalibrating-e-commerce-compliance-across-the-indian-data-privacy-framework/)
- Rule 4(4) amended: every e-commerce entity must prominently display legal name, headquarters and branch addresses, website details, and email, landline and mobile for customer care and the grievance officer. — [S.S. Rana](https://ssrana.in/articles/consumer-protection-e-commerce-amendment-rules-2026-recalibrating-e-commerce-compliance-across-the-indian-data-privacy-framework/)
- Rule 4(5) amended: the grievance officer must acknowledge within 48 hours, give the complainant a copy of the recorded complaint, and resolve within one month. — [S.S. Rana](https://ssrana.in/articles/consumer-protection-e-commerce-amendment-rules-2026-recalibrating-e-commerce-compliance-across-the-indian-data-privacy-framework/)
- New Rule 4(15): comply with the 2023 Dark Patterns Guidelines, do a self-audit every year, and prominently display a compliance certificate. — [S.S. Rana](https://ssrana.in/articles/consumer-protection-e-commerce-amendment-rules-2026-recalibrating-e-commerce-compliance-across-the-indian-data-privacy-framework/)
- Platforms must become partners in the NCH convergence process. — [LiveLaw](https://www.livelaw.in/consumer-cases/government-amends-e-commerce-rules-to-strengthen-consumer-protection-550184)
- Price reductions must show both the reduced price and the prior price. The prior price is the lowest price in the preceding 30 days. Search results must not be manipulated to mislead, and sponsored listings must be clearly marked. — [LiveLaw](https://www.livelaw.in/consumer-cases/government-amends-e-commerce-rules-to-strengthen-consumer-protection-550184)
- Disclosures are strengthened for best-before dates, return/refund policy, warranty, delivery and payment details. For imports, importer details and country of origin must be disclosed. — [LiveLaw](https://www.livelaw.in/consumer-cases/government-amends-e-commerce-rules-to-strengthen-consumer-protection-550184)
- New Rule 5(6) (marketplaces only): no using collected data to push own-brand goods or imply seller association without express, affirmative consent. Marketplaces also may not collect bundled fees for unrelated services (membership/loyalty programmes exempt). Not relevant to a pure D2C store unless it hosts third-party sellers. — [S.S. Rana](https://ssrana.in/articles/consumer-protection-e-commerce-amendment-rules-2026-recalibrating-e-commerce-compliance-across-the-indian-data-privacy-framework/); [LiveLaw](https://www.livelaw.in/consumer-cases/government-amends-e-commerce-rules-to-strengthen-consumer-protection-550184)
- The notified amendment text and penalty provisions were not seen. I found no 2025 amendment to these Rules. The 2021 draft amendment (flash-sale limits) is a draft that was not enacted. — [IPleaders](https://blog.ipleaders.in/proposed-amendments-consumer-protection-e-commerce-rules-2020/) (context only)

**Core 2020 duties of every e-commerce entity (summary source, rule numbers not given)**
- Be registered in India (or have an Indian presence) and appoint a contact person in India. — [IndiaLaw](https://www.indialaw.in/blog/civil/consumer-protection-e-commerce-rules/)
- Display legal name, headquarters/branch addresses, website, and support email and phone. — [IndiaLaw](https://www.indialaw.in/blog/civil/consumer-protection-e-commerce-rules/)
- Appoint a grievance officer with visible details (48 hours to acknowledge, one month to resolve). — [IndiaLaw](https://www.indialaw.in/blog/civil/consumer-protection-e-commerce-rules/)
- No unfair trade practices, price manipulation for unfair profit, or discrimination among consumers of the same class. — [IndiaLaw](https://www.indialaw.in/blog/civil/consumer-protection-e-commerce-rules/)
- Consent to a purchase must be explicit and affirmative, with no pre-ticked boxes (Rule 4(9)). — [TaxGuru on CCPA advisory](https://taxguru.in/corporate-law/ccpa-mandates-e-commerce-self-audits-dark-patterns.html)
- No cancellation charge unless the entity bears similar charges when it cancels. — [IndiaLaw](https://www.indialaw.in/blog/civil/consumer-protection-e-commerce-rules/)
- Process refunds promptly in line with RBI rules. Cooperate with NCH. Disclose the importer's name for imported goods. — [IndiaLaw](https://www.indialaw.in/blog/civil/consumer-protection-e-commerce-rules/)

**Inventory entity (own-stock D2C store) display duties (the page labels this section without rule number; commonly Rule 6/7, verify)**
- Display returns, refunds, exchanges, warranties, delivery and shipping costs, payment methods, and complaint resolution. — [IndiaLaw](https://www.indialaw.in/blog/civil/consumer-protection-e-commerce-rules/)
- Display mandatory legal notices, payment security information, cancellation procedure and fees. — [IndiaLaw](https://www.indialaw.in/blog/civil/consumer-protection-e-commerce-rules/)
- Display the total price with a breakup of delivery, taxes and other charges, and a complaint ticket number for tracking. — [IndiaLaw](https://www.indialaw.in/blog/civil/consumer-protection-e-commerce-rules/)
- No fake reviews or misrepresentation of quality. Ads must match the goods' real characteristics. — [IndiaLaw](https://www.indialaw.in/blog/civil/consumer-protection-e-commerce-rules/)
- Must not refuse return or refund for defective or non-conforming goods or late delivery, except force majeure. If you claim goods are authentic, you answer for authenticity. — [IndiaLaw](https://www.indialaw.in/blog/civil/consumer-protection-e-commerce-rules/)

**Marketplace/seller extras (only if you list third-party sellers):** seller details with ratings, ticket number, ranking explanation, sellers' GSTIN and PAN, and written contract. — [IndiaLaw](https://www.indialaw.in/blog/civil/consumer-protection-e-commerce-rules/)

**Penalty:** Violations of the Rules are enforced under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 (see section 2). No separate penalty is in the Rules. — [IndiaLaw](https://www.indialaw.in/blog/civil/consumer-protection-e-commerce-rules/)

### Inferences
- For a small D2C shop, the practical checklist is: footer/contact page with legal name and addresses, a named grievance officer with a 48-hour/one-month promise, a policies page, a price breakup at checkout, country of origin on each product, no pre-ticked boxes, and no fake reviews or fake timers.
- Because the 2026 amendment starts 1 Jan 2027, a shop launching after that date needs the dark-pattern self-audit certificate and NCH registration at launch.
- "Prior price = lowest in the last 30 days" means "was Rs X" strikethrough prices need price-history records.

### Gaps
- Exact notified text, G.S.R. number, and penalty clauses of the 2026 amendment were not retrieved.
- Exact rule numbers for inventory-entity duties were not confirmed.
- Whether NCH registration or the compliance certificate has a prescribed format is unknown.
- Flash-sale ban: not found in the enacted 2020 Rules (only in a 2021 draft).

## 2. Consumer Protection Act, 2019 (product liability, UTP, misleading ads, CCPA powers, penalties)

### Takeaway
The CCPA can order recalls, refunds and discontinuation, and fine up to Rs 10 lakh (Rs 50 lakh for repeats) for misleading ads and UTPs. Courts can impose jail and fines under s.89. Sellers also face product-liability claims.

### Cited Findings
- Misleading advertisement: the CCPA can fine manufacturers, advertisers and endorsers up to Rs 10 lakh, rising to Rs 50 lakh for repeats. It can bar an endorser for up to 1 year (3 years for repeats). — [secondary summary](https://www.iiprd.com/tag/ccpa/) (single weak source; the figures match the Act's s.21 as I recall, see Gaps)
- Section 89 (court-imposed offence): false or misleading ad prejudicial to consumers, up to 2 years' imprisonment and fine up to Rs 10 lakh; repeat offence up to 5 years and Rs 50 lakh. — [Bar and Bench](https://www.barandbench.com/view-point/dark-patterns-in-india-from-hidden-practices-to-regulatory-spotlight); [Juscorpus overview](https://www.juscorpus.com/offences-and-penalties-under-the-consumer-protection-act-an-overview/)
- Sections 90 and 91 cover adulterated and spurious goods. — [PRS Bill text](https://PRSINDIA.org/files/bills_acts/bills_parliament/2019/THE%20CONSUMER%20PROTECTION%20BILL,%202019%20Bill%20Text.pdf)
- Product liability is Chapter VI: manufacturer (s.84), service provider (s.85), seller (s.86). Claims go to the consumer commissions. — [PRS Bill text](https://PRSINDIA.org/files/bills_acts/bills_parliament/2019/THE%20CONSUMER%20PROTECTION%20BILL,%202019%20Bill%20Text.pdf)
- Unfair trade practice is defined at s.2(47); misleading advertisement at s.2(28). False representation about the need or usefulness of goods is an UTP. — [Lawful Legal](https://lawfullegal.in/unfair-trade-practices-in-india-under-consumer-protection-act-2019-introduction-definition-examples-remedies-and-cases/)
- CCPA enforcement uses ss.20-21 read with ss.10 and 18. Recent orders: PhysicsWallah Rs 5 lakh (1 Jun 2026), McAfee Rs 1 lakh (20 May 2026), Rapido Rs 10 lakh. About 7 platforms penalised, around Rs 20 lakh total. — [MediaNama](https://www.medianama.com/2026/06/223-consumer-protection-authority-fines-physicswallah-and-mcafee-for-dark-patterns-what-counts-as-consent-now/); [Bar and Bench](https://www.barandbench.com/law-firms/view-point/from-nudge-to-notice-what-indias-dark-pattern-orders-mean-for-digital-business)
- CCPA Guidelines for Prevention of Misleading Advertisements and Endorsements, 9 June 2022, apply to all ads. — [IIPRD](https://www.iiprd.com/tag/ccpa/)

### Inferences
- Sources disagree on whether the Rs 10 lakh / Rs 50 lakh figures sit under s.21 (CCPA, administrative) or s.89 (criminal). Likely both exist: s.21 for the CCPA's penalty and s.89 for prosecution. Treat both as live risks.
- Sellers are within product-liability reach (s.86), so keep supplier and batch records.

### Gaps
- Statutory text of ss.84-86, 21 and 88 not retrieved directly. [UNVERIFIED] statements: CCPA can order recall, refunds and discontinuation (s.20), and non-compliance with CCPA directions is punishable (s.88/s.89).
- Seller-liability conditions in s.86 not confirmed.

## 3. CCPA Dark Patterns Guidelines, 2023 and the self-audit directive

### Takeaway
Thirteen specified dark patterns are banned for all platforms. The CCPA advisory of 5 June 2025 told platforms to self-audit within 3 months. From 1 Jan 2027 the e-commerce Rules make an annual audit and visible certificate mandatory.

### Cited Findings
- Issued 30 Nov 2023 under s.18 of the Act. — [Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/digital/ccpa-warns-e-comm-firms-over-dark-patterns-self-audit-within-3-months-69249.htm)
- The 13 patterns (Annexure 1) — [Bar and Bench](https://www.barandbench.com/view-point/dark-patterns-in-india-from-hidden-practices-to-regulatory-spotlight):
  1. False urgency (fake scarcity or popularity)
  2. Basket sneaking (items added at checkout without consent; free items and necessary charges excluded)
  3. Confirm shaming (guilt-trip opt-out wording)
  4. Forced action (forcing extras or personal data)
  5. Subscription trap (card required for free item, hard to cancel)
  6. Interface interference (misdirecting design)
  7. Bait and switch (cheap offer unavailable, pricier substitute)
  8. Drip pricing (charges revealed late)
  9. Disguised advertisement (undisclosed paid promotion, including influencers)
  10. Nagging (repeated prompts after refusal)
  11. Trick question (confusing wording)
  12. SaaS billing (hidden or forced recurring payments)
  13. Rogue malware (fake virus warnings)
- Advisory 5 June 2025: self-audit within 3 months; platforms encouraged to file self-declarations. Flipkart and bigbasket reported completing audits (Oct 2025). — [TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/corporate-law/ccpa-mandates-e-commerce-self-audits-dark-patterns.html); [Storyboard18](https://storyboard18.com/how-it-works/bigbasket-completes-self-audit-to-comply-with-ccpa-guidelines-on-dark-patterns-82062.htm)
- Penalties: see section 2 (ss.20-21 monetary penalties in orders; s.89 criminal exposure per one source). — [MediaNama](https://www.medianama.com/2026/06/223-consumer-protection-authority-fines-physicswallah-and-mcafee-for-dark-patterns-what-counts-as-consent-now/)

### Inferences
- Common fashion-site risks: countdown timers, "only 2 left" banners that are not real, pre-selected add-ons or gift wrap, shipping fee only at the last step, "No, I don't like discounts" decline buttons, hard-to-find unsubscribe.

### Gaps
- Exact guideline wording and carve-outs not read from the primary document.

## 4. Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011 and e-commerce

### Takeaway
Garments sold pre-packed are "packaged commodities". Rule 6 declarations must appear on the pack and, for online sales, on the product page. Penalties are monetary and can include jail.

### Cited Findings
- Rule 6(10) (some sources say 4(10) in the 2017 draft; conflict) requires e-commerce entities to display Rule 6(1) declarations online: manufacturer/packer/importer name and address, country of origin, generic name, net quantity, manufacture date, best-before/expiry where applicable, and MRP inclusive of all taxes. Month/year of packing may be excluded per one source. — [S.S. Rana](https://ssrana.in/articles/compliances-for-website-e-commerce-website/); [Lakshmikumaran](https://lakshmisri.com/insights/articles/aiming-greater-transparency-on-sale-of-packaged-commodity-online)
- MRP must be in Indian currency, inclusive of all taxes. 2017 amendments (effective 1 Jan 2018) extended coverage to e-commerce. — [Lakshmikumaran](https://lakshmisri.com/insights/articles/aiming-greater-transparency-on-sale-of-packaged-commodity-online)
- 2026 amendments (Feb and April 2026) tighten e-commerce disclosures, centred on country of origin visible before the transaction is completed. Some provisions of the Second Amendment Rules, 2026 apply from 1 July 2027 (provisions not specified). — [Mondaq / Khurana and Khurana](https://www.mondaq.com/india/dodd-frank-consumer-protection-act/1806934/legal-metrology-packaged-commodities-amendment-rules-2026-enhancing-transparency-and-consumer-protection-in-e-commerce)
- 2025 amendments (medical devices, pan masala) are not relevant to apparel. — [Drishti IAS](https://www.drishtiias.com/daily-updates/daily-news-analysis/legal-metrology-packaged-commodities-amendment-rules-2025/print_manually); [TeamLease RegTech](https://teamleaseregtech.com/updates/article/50220/legal-metrology-packaged-commodities-second-amendment-rules-2025/)
- Penalties: s.36(1) Legal Metrology Act 2009 - non-conforming pre-packed goods, fine up to Rs 25,000 first offence, Rs 50,000 second, Rs 50,000 to Rs 1 lakh later, up to 1 year jail for repeats; net-quantity errors fine Rs 10,000 to Rs 50,000+. Offences compoundable (s.48). Residual Rule 32: Rs 5,000 fine. — [S.S. Rana](https://ssrana.in/corporate-laws/legal-metrology-and-packaging/legal-metrology-notice-india/); [Lawx s.36](https://lawx.in/acts/section/47/480/4622)

### Inferences
- Pack/label for each garment should carry: product name, net quantity (1 piece / dimensions), MRP incl. taxes, month/year of manufacture or packing, maker/packer/importer name and address, consumer care phone/email, country of origin. Online: same on the product page.

### Gaps
- Consumer-care declaration is in the Rules (Rule 6(1)(e)) per my recollection, not confirmed by fetched sources [UNVERIFIED].
- Exact 2026 amendment text and the 1 July 2027 provisions unknown. Whether textiles have size/dimension rules beyond net quantity was not confirmed.

## 5. Textile/apparel specifics: BIS/QCO, labelling, care labels, sizes

### Takeaway
Ordinary fashion apparel needs no BIS mark. Mandatory fibre/care labelling is proposed but not yet notified as of the sources found.

### Cited Findings
- Finished garments (e.g. blouses, shirts, category 314) are not under mandatory BIS certification; protective items like high-visibility clothing (IS 15809:2017) are. — [Alibaba seller blog](https://seller.alibaba.com/blogs/2026/southeast-asia/apparel/bis-certification-indian-market-compliance-guide-alibaba) (non-official, low reliability)
- IS 12675:1989 (garment labelling: fibre, care, size, maker, origin) is a voluntary reference. IS 14853:2000 and IS 14854:2000 are size designation standards, not mandatory. — [law.resource.org IS 12675](https://law.resource.org/pub/in/bis/S12/is.12675.1989.pdf); [IS 14853](https://law.resource.org/pub/in/bis/S12/is.14853.2000.pdf)
- Textile (Consumer Protection) Regulations, 1988: per a 2000 Lok Sabha answer, finished fabric must carry maker name/address, fibre composition and packing month/year. — [Lok Sabha reply](https://eparlib.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/427737/1/4627.pdf) (old; applicability to garments unconfirmed)
- Dec 2025 reports: Ministry of Textiles finalising mandatory labelling for apparel and home textiles (exact fibre %, country of origin, care instructions), with no confirmed date. — [Apparel Resources](https://apparelresources.com/business-news/policy/india-reportedly-planning-mandatory-labelling-apparel-home-textiles); [EcoTextile](https://www.ecotextile.com/2025121861159/radar/india-set-to-announce-mandatory-labelling-rules-for-textiles-and-apparel/)
- Other BIS compulsory registration items (toys, electronics, helmets, footwear-related) may touch accessories. Not researched in detail.

### Inferences
- Best practice is to add fibre content, care instructions, size chart and country of origin voluntarily now; this also satisfies the e-commerce Rules' product-information duty and prepares for the expected labelling rule.

### Gaps
- No official confirmation of whether mandatory labelling was notified in 2026.
- Footwear, leather, bags, jewellery and any apparel/accessory QCOs (e.g. footwear QCO) not verified. Check BIS Compulsory Registration list for each accessory category.
- Whether the 1988 Textile regulations still bind garment sellers is unverified.

## 6. Advertising rules (ASCI, influencers, claims, price comparisons)

### Takeaway
ASCI is self-regulatory, but the CCPA 2022 endorsement guidelines and the dark-pattern "disguised ad" ban give legal force. Disclose paid influencer content clearly and substantiate claims.

### Cited Findings
- ASCI Influencer Guidelines (May 2021, updated): disclose any material connection with labels such as ad, sponsored, collaboration, partnership, free gift, affiliate. Instagram's Paid Partnership tag alone is insufficient. Disclosure goes at the start of captions, and on-image or on-screen for set durations. Brands share responsibility. Health/finance addendum 2025. — [GryNow](https://www.grynow.in/blog/asci-guidelines-for-influencer-marketing-in-india.html) (secondary)
- ASCI itself can request edits or withdrawal and publicly name offenders; it has no statutory sanction. — [GryNow](https://www.grynow.in/blog/asci-guidelines-for-influencer-marketing-in-india.html); [Bar and Bench](https://www.barandbench.com/view-point/dark-patterns-in-india-from-hidden-practices-to-regulatory-spotlight)
- CCPA 2022 Guidelines on Misleading Ads and Endorsements; penalties in section 2. — [IIPRD](https://www.iiprd.com/tag/ccpa/)
- Under the 2026 amendment, strike-through discounts must reference the 30-day lowest price (section 1).

### Inferences
- Avoid unsubstantiated claims ("100% pure", "sustainable", "handmade"), and keep proof for "original price" and "limited stock".

### Gaps
- Primary ASCI code chapters (general claims, comparative ads, price claims) and the CCPA 2022 text not read.

## 7. Returns, refunds, cancellation: legal duty vs business choice

### Takeaway
The law does not give a general "no-questions" return right in India. You must refund or replace defective, misdescribed or late goods and publish your policy. Anything beyond that (size exchange, change of mind) is your choice, but must be clearly stated and honoured.

### Cited Findings
- Sellers/inventory entities may not refuse returns or refunds for defective, deficient, spurious or non-conforming goods, or late delivery (except force majeure). — [IndiaLaw](https://www.indialaw.in/blog/civil/consumer-protection-e-commerce-rules/)
- Policies on returns, refunds, exchange, warranty, delivery and shipping cost must be displayed. — [IndiaLaw](https://www.indialaw.in/blog/civil/consumer-protection-e-commerce-rules/)
- Cancellation charges are barred unless you pay similar charges when you cancel; refunds follow RBI rules. — [IndiaLaw](https://www.indialaw.in/blog/civil/consumer-protection-e-commerce-rules/)
- Consumer data/consent must be explicit and affirmative (Rule 4(9)). — [TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/corporate-law/ccpa-mandates-e-commerce-self-audits-dark-patterns.html)

### Inferences
- A "no returns/exchange only" policy for non-defective items is lawful if disclosed upfront, but wrongly refusing to refund defective or wrong-item orders is a violation. Be consistent between the site text and practice.
