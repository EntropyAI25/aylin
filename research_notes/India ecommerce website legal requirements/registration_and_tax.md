# India D2C e-commerce website (apparel/accessories): registration and tax requirements, as of Oct 2026

Method note: sources were mostly secondary commentary (CA/tax blogs, vendor guides). I could not open primary notifications on cbic-gst.gov.in, incometax.gov.in or dgft.gov.in. Items marked "verify" need checking against the primary text.

## Business structure, Udyam/MSME, other registrations

### Takeaway
Udyam registration is optional paperwork that is useful for the cheaper trademark fee. The search found no source on choice of structure, Shop and Establishment, trade licence, or current account. Those are covered only as background knowledge in Gaps.

### Cited Findings
- Revised MSME limits (secondary sources, effective 1 Apr 2025, gazette dated 21 Mar 2025): micro = investment up to Rs 2.5 crore and turnover up to Rs 10 crore; small = Rs 25 crore / Rs 100 crore; medium = Rs 125 crore / Rs 500 crore. Export sales are excluded from the turnover calculation. — [Taxmann](https://www.taxmann.com/post/blog/revised-msme-classification), [SetIndiaBiz](https://www.setindiabiz.com/blog/msme-definition)
- The sources conflict on whether the test is "either investment or turnover" or "both"; check the gazette text. — [Taxmann](https://www.taxmann.com/post/blog/revised-msme-classification)
- A Udyam certificate lets you pay the lower trademark fee (Rs 4,500 per class instead of Rs 9,000). — [Intepat](https://intepat.com/blog/trademark-registration-fees-india)

### Inferences
- A new shop with little plant and machinery and under Rs 10 crore turnover is a micro enterprise. Udyam registration is free on udyamregistration.gov.in (fee-free status not confirmed in this research).

### Gaps
- No sourced material on sole proprietorship vs partnership vs LLP vs Pvt Ltd, MCA fees, Shop and Establishment (state-specific), trade licence (municipal), or current bank account/PAN requirements. Background knowledge only, not verified here: a proprietorship needs just PAN plus GST plus a bank account; a Pvt Ltd or LLP gives limited liability but adds annual ROC compliance and audit. Confirm with a CA.

## GST registration: mandatory or threshold-based, own website, inter-state

### Takeaway
A seller selling only its own goods through its own website is not an "e-commerce operator" and is not caught by the Section 24 compulsory-registration rule. The normal turnover threshold applies: Rs 40 lakh for goods-only suppliers (state-dependent, lower in some states), otherwise Rs 20 lakh. Registration is compulsory from the first sale if you also sell through marketplaces that collect TCS. In practice, payment gateways and shipping aggregators may ask for a GSTIN.

### Cited Findings
- Commentary says a seller on its own site, taking payment through a gateway, is outside compulsory registration under Section 24, and Section 52 TCS does not apply to it. The normal threshold then applies. — [TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/goods-and-service-tax/applicability-gst-online-business-services.html), [ComplianceCalendar](https://www.compliancecalendar.in/learn/gst-registration-for-e-commerce-sellers)
- Section 22 threshold: goods-only suppliers up to Rs 40 lakh (state-wise limits and conditions apply); otherwise generally Rs 20 lakh, lower in some states. Aggregate turnover is counted across the same PAN, including offline sales. — [ComplianceCalendar](https://www.compliancecalendar.in/learn/gst-registration-for-e-commerce-sellers)
- Section 24 compulsory registration covers suppliers selling goods through an e-commerce operator that must collect TCS under Section 52. — [ComplianceCalendar](https://www.compliancecalendar.in/learn/gst-registration-for-e-commerce-sellers)
- Notification 34/2023-Central Tax (effective 1 Oct 2023) exempts small goods suppliers using e-commerce operators, but only if they make no inter-State supply and sell in only one State or UT. — [ComplianceCalendar](https://www.compliancecalendar.in/learn/gst-registration-for-e-commerce-sellers), [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/amp/story/news/exempt-small-suppliers-making-inter-state-supplies-on-e-commerce-platforms-from-gst-registration-gtri-news-301848)
- The Rs 40 lakh goods limit comes from Notification 10/2019-Central Tax (7 Mar 2019). Notification 10/2017-Integrated Tax (inter-State supply exemption) covers services only, up to Rs 20 lakh (Rs 10 lakh in special category states). Inter-State goods supply is therefore not covered by that exemption. — [TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/goods-and-service-tax/gst-registration-exemption-upto-%E2%82%B940-lakhs-riders.html), [GSTZen](https://gstzen.in/a/integrated-tax-notification-102017-seeks-to-exempt-persons-making-inter-state-supplies-of-taxable-services-from-registration-under-section-232.html), [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/amp/story/news/exempt-small-suppliers-making-inter-state-supplies-on-e-commerce-platforms-from-gst-registration-gtri-news-301848)
- One article says suppliers of taxable goods making inter-state supply must register irrespective of turnover under Section 24. This is consistent with the point above. — [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/amp/story/news/exempt-small-suppliers-making-inter-state-supplies-on-e-commerce-platforms-from-gst-registration-gtri-news-301848)
- A multi-seller site that collects payment for third parties may be an ECO, with TCS collection and GSTR-8. — [ComplianceCalendar](https://www.compliancecalendar.in/learn/gst-registration-for-e-commerce-sellers)
- Practical point: Indian payment gateways may require a GSTIN to settle funds even for own-website sales (a practical requirement, not a Section 24 rule). — [TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/goods-and-service-tax/applicability-gst-online-business-services.html)
- Simplified registration, Rule 14A (Notification 18/2025-Central Tax, effective 1 Nov 2025): optional route for applicants whose expected monthly output tax on B2B supplies is Rs 2.5 lakh or less. Registration is granted within 3 working days of the ARN after Aadhaar authentication. Only one such registration is allowed per PAN per State. Withdrawal is via REG-32/REG-33; the filing-history condition changed from 1 Apr 2026. — [Tally](https://tallysolutions.com/gst/gst-rule-9a-14a-three-day-registration/), [GST Council newsletter](https://www.gstcouncil.gov.in/sites/default/files/2025-11/october_issue_0.pdf)
- Returns (QRMP, turnover up to Rs 5 crore): GSTR-1 quarterly by the 13th after quarter end; GSTR-3B quarterly by the 22nd or 24th depending on state; tax for months 1 and 2 paid via PMT-06 by the 25th; optional IFF by the 13th. Above Rs 5 crore: monthly GSTR-1 by the 11th and GSTR-3B by the 20th. Composition filers: GSTR-4 annually by 30 April, CMP-08 quarterly by the 18th. These sources are old calendars; verify. — [Taxscan](https://www.taxscan.in/?p=124665), [Patron Accounting](https://www.patronaccounting.com/tools/gst-return-due-date-tracker)
- Late fee: one source cites Rs 50/day (Rs 25 CGST + Rs 25 SGST) for returns with tax liability, plus 18% interest on late tax. Verify. — [Taxscan](https://www.taxscan.in/?p=124665)

### Inferences
- Plan: if you will ship across state lines (the normal case for a D2C store), the Rs 40 lakh exemption is of limited use for marketplace sales. For own-website sales the Section 24 trigger does not apply, but many practitioners recommend registering anyway. This is an inference from the sources, not a sourced statement.
- A seller on both the own site and marketplaces such as Amazon, Myntra or Meesho must register from day one.
- The composition scheme (limit about Rs 1.5 crore per one tracker) was not verified for e-commerce or inter-state sellers; treat it as unavailable until checked.

### Gaps
- Primary text of Sections 22, 24 and 52 and the current notifications not opened.
- Composition scheme eligibility for inter-State or e-commerce sellers: not found. Background knowledge only, unverified here: composition is barred for inter-State supply and for e-commerce operator sales.
- Place of supply for online sales: not researched. Background (unverified): for goods it is the location where delivery ends, so IGST applies for out-of-state delivery and CGST+SGST within the state.
- GST invoice contents (Rule 46): not sourced. Background only: supplier name, address, GSTIN, invoice number and date, HSN, description, quantity, taxable value, tax rate and amount, place of supply.
- Any 2026 GST registration changes beyond Rule 14A: none found.

## HSN codes and GST rates for apparel and footwear (GST 2.0)

### Takeaway
Since 22 Sep 2025, apparel and footwear priced up to Rs 2,500 per piece or pair attract 5% GST, and above that 18%. Taxpayers up to Rs 5 crore need a 4-digit HSN on B2B invoices; 6 digits are needed above Rs 5 crore.

### Cited Findings
- From 22 Sep 2025, garments up to Rs 2,500 per piece are taxed at 5% (the earlier 5% limit was Rs 1,000). Garments above Rs 2,500 are at 18% (earlier 12%). Footwear follows the same Rs 2,500 per pair threshold. Implemented by Central Tax (Rate) Notification 9/2025 and Integrated Tax (Rate) Notification 9/2025 dated 17 Sep 2025. — [Bajaj Finserv](https://www.bajajfinserv.in/gst-on-clothes), [Bajaj Finserv footwear](https://www.bajajfinserv.in/gst-on-footwear), [Council for Leather Exports](https://leatherindia.org/gst-rate-reduction-for-certain-products/), [Taxscan on 56th Council](https://www.taxscan.in/top-stories/key-highlights-of-the-56th-gst-council-meeting-rate-rationalization-trade-facilitation-1432230)
- It is unclear whether the Rs 2,500 limit is tax-exclusive. Only forum opinions were found (one says exclusive of tax). — [TaxTMI forum](https://www.taxtmi.com/forum/issue?id=120496)
- HSN: up to Rs 5 crore turnover requires 4 digits on B2B invoices (optional on B2C); above Rs 5 crore requires 6 digits on both. Rule from Notification 78/2020-CT (15 Oct 2020), effective 1 Apr 2021. Reporting more digits than required is allowed. — [GST Council press release](https://gstcouncil.gov.in/sites/default/files/2024-02/press_release_hs_code_sac_issue.pdf), [Taxscan](https://www.taxscan.in/?p=104424), [ClearTax](https://news.cleartax.in/?p=6089)
- One source (Taxmann) says above Rs 5 crore needs only 4 digits; this conflicts with the official release and is likely an error. — [Taxmann](https://www.taxmann.com/post/blog/what-is-hsn-code-under-gst)

### Inferences
- Because the rate depends on the per-piece price, products on either side of Rs 2,500 will need different tax rates in the store's tax settings. The treatment of discounts, bundles and the effect on the price shown to customers needs a CA's confirmation.

### Gaps
- Rates for accessories (bags, belts, jewellery, sunglasses) were not researched; they differ by item.
- Specific HSN codes (for example 6109, 6204, 6403) were not sourced; verify on the GST portal.

## TCS under Section 52, TDS and income tax, professional tax

### Takeaway
GST TCS applies only if you sell through a marketplace; it is not charged on your own-website sales. For income tax, the Income-tax Act 2025 applies from 1 Apr 2026, and presumptive taxation sits in Section 58.

### Cited Findings
- GST TCS under Section 52 is 0.5% of net taxable value in total (0.25% CGST + 0.25% SGST intra-State; 0.5% IGST inter-State), effective as quoted from 10 Jul 2024. It has no turnover threshold. It is a credit against your GST liability and not an extra tax. — [ComplianceCalendar](https://www.compliancecalendar.in/learn/gst-registration-for-e-commerce-sellers), [GSTGyaan](https://gstgyaan.com/summary-of-notifications-issued-us-52%281%29-of-cgst-act-2017). Conflict: [TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/?p=1062722) gives 0.5% for intra-State; other exam material gives an older 1% IGST structure.
- Income-tax Act 2025 is effective 1 Apr 2026 (tax year 2026-27) and replaces the 1961 Act. Sections 44AD/44ADA/44AE are folded into Section 58. — [TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/income-tax/presumptive-taxation-residents-1961-act-via-a-vis-2025-act.html)
- Section 58 business presumptive scheme: turnover up to Rs 2 crore, or up to Rs 3 crore where cash receipts are at most 5%; deemed profit is 6% of receipts through banking or digital modes and 8% of other receipts, or actual profit if higher. Declaring lower income triggers a tax audit. Losses and deductions cannot be set off. — [TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/income-tax/analysis-presumptive-taxation-section-58-income-tax-act-2025.html), [TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/?p=1005485)
- Under the 1961 Act, a person opting for 44AD paid all advance tax in one instalment by 15 March. — [Income Tax Dept](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/w/tax-on-presumptive-basis-in-case-of-certain-businesses)

### Inferences
- A sole proprietor with a clothing store under Rs 2 crore may use the presumptive scheme (6% digital) and avoid books and audit; this needs a CA's confirmation, given the new Act.
- Marketplace TCS is claimed as a credit when you file GSTR-3B.

### Gaps
- TDS under 194-O (ECO deducting on sellers' payments), its current rate, and whether the 2025 Act renames or changes it: not found.
- Advance tax under the 2025 Act for Section 58 filers: not confirmed.
- Professional tax: not researched. It is state-specific, and many states levy it on salaried staff and some on proprietors; check your state.
- Income tax slab rates and the ITR form for businesses: not researched.

## Exports: IEC, LUT, FEMA/RBI

### Takeaway
For commercial exports an IEC appears to be required, and exports of goods are zero-rated, so you either pay IGST and claim a refund or ship under an LUT without paying tax.

### Cited Findings
- IEC is a 10-digit DGFT code, lifetime validity, with a mandatory annual update between April and June as claimed by guides. DHL India says an IEC is mandatory for commercial exports and only bona fide personal shipments are exempt. — [Shiprocket](https://www.shiprocket.in/blog/import-export-code-iec-india/), [DHL KYC](https://www.dhl.com/content/dam/dhl/local/in/dhl-express/documents/pdf/KYC%20Norms.pdf)
- Courier export cap of Rs 10 lakh per consignment (DHL, Dec 2025); a later DHL page says the cap was removed in early 2026. Unverified from a primary source. — [DHL](https://www.dhl.com/discover/en-in/ship-with-dhl/export-with-dhl/required-export-documents)
- LUT is filed on the GST portal in Form RFD-11 and is valid for one financial year. Export must happen within 3 months of the invoice date (Rule 96A). Failure to export or realise payment can lead to LUT withdrawal and demands with interest. — [Razorpay](https://razorpay.com/blog/lut-in-gst/), [PayGlocal](https://payglocal.in/blog/mastering-exports-lut-under-gst)
- Services exports need payment in convertible foreign exchange within 1 year from the invoice date. — [Razorpay](https://razorpay.com/blog/lut-in-gst/)

### Gaps
- FEMA realisation period for goods exports (background: 9 months, per RBI; not verified here), AD bank requirements, and payment gateway rules for cross-border e-commerce: not sourced.
- Whether IEC can be avoided for low-value courier exports: not found. No source supports that.

## Penalties for operating without GST registration

### Takeaway
Sources agree on a penalty of the higher of Rs 10,000 or 10% of tax due for failing to register, plus tax and interest. The exact statutory basis and wording differ between sources, so verify in the CGST Act.

### Cited Findings
- Penalty for non-registration: Rs 10,000 or 10% of tax due, whichever is higher; tax, interest and penalty are decided through demand proceedings under Section 73/74, or Section 74A for FY 2024-25 onward. Additional consequences include loss of ITC and detention or confiscation of goods. — [Masters India](https://www.mastersindia.co/blog/penalty-for-not-registering-or-late-registering-under-gst/), [Kanakkupillai](https://kanakkupillai.com/learn/penalty-for-non-compliance-with-gst)
- Section 122(1) is described elsewhere as Rs 10,000 or the tax evaded, whichever is higher, which would be larger for deliberate evasion. The sub-clause cited for non-registration varies (122(1)(xi) vs 122(1)(ii)). — [TaxTMI](https://www.taxtmi.com/article/detailed?id=10386), [Kanakkupillai](https://www.kanakkupillai.com/learn/penalty-for-not-registering-for-gst-when-required/)
- Section 125 residual penalty is up to Rs 25,000. — [iPleaders](https://blog.ipleaders.in/gst-non-compliance/)

### Gaps
- Prosecution thresholds (for example tax evasion above Rs 2 crore) not researched.

## Trademark, brand and domain

### Takeaway
File a Class 25 (clothing/footwear) trademark early; the government fee is Rs 4,500 per class for individuals, startups and MSMEs and Rs 9,000 for others.

### Cited Findings
- E-filing fee is Rs 4,500 per class for individuals, DPIIT startups and Udyam MSMEs and Rs 9,000 for companies/LLPs (guide updated 16 Mar 2026). Startups need a DPIIT certificate and MSMEs a Udyam certificate. — [Intepat](https://intepat.com/blog/trademark-registration-fees-india)
- Registration timeline is 6 to 12 months (vendor estimate); validity is 10 years; renewal fee about Rs 9,000 to 10,000. — [Patron Accounting](https://patronaccounting.com/trademark-registration), [Global Law Experts](https://globallawexperts.com/how-much-does-a-trademark-registration-cost-in-india/)

### Gaps
- Class 25 mapping and official IP India fee schedule not confirmed (check ipindiaonline.gov.in).
- Domain registration rules (.in via NIXI, domain disputes) not researched.
- Other website legal requirements (Consumer Protection E-Commerce Rules 2020 disclosures, privacy policy, DPDP Act) are out of this note's scope.
