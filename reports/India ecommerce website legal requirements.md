# Online shop ko legal-safe banao: India go-live guide

**Chhoti si baat pehle: koi bhi source "zero legal issue" ki guarantee nahi de sakta, aur ye report mostly secondary sources (law-firm blogs, CA/tax blogs, vendor guides) par based hai.** Primary gazette text research mein khola nahi gaya. Phir bhi picture clear hai. Fashion/accessories ka own-stock D2C store jo website aur WhatsApp se bechta hai, uske liye 5 cheezein sabse zaroori hain. (1) GST registration, kyunki doosre state mein bhejna hai. (2) Payments sirf licensed payment gateway ya business account se lena, personal UPI se nahi. (3) Website par seller ki poori details, grievance officer aur return/refund policy dikhana. (4) Fake timer, fake "sale" price, fake reviews jaise dark patterns bilkul nahi. (5) Privacy policy aur customer data ki basic security. Teen badi deadlines aane wali hain: **E-Commerce Amendment Rules 2026 ka 1 Jan 2027**, **DPDP ke main duties ka ~13/14 May 2027**, aur **Legal Metrology ke kuch provisions ka 1 July 2027**. Neeche har point ke saath bataya hai kaunsa pakka hai aur kaunsa CA/lawyer se confirm karna hai. Ye legal advice nahi hai.

## Business basics: GST, tax aur Udyam

**GST sabse bada sawaal hai, aur notes mein ek andar ki conflict bhi hai.** Ek taraf commentary kehti hai ki jo seller sirf apni website par apna maal bechta hai, wo "e-commerce operator" nahi hai aur Section 24 ke compulsory-registration rule mein nahi aata, to normal threshold lagta hai: goods ke liye **Rs 40 lakh** (kuch states mein kam) ([TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/goods-and-service-tax/applicability-gst-online-business-services.html), [ComplianceCalendar](https://www.compliancecalendar.in/learn/gst-registration-for-e-commerce-sellers)). Doosri taraf wahi notes batate hain ki inter-State supply wali exemption (Notification 10/2017-Integrated Tax) sirf services ke liye hai, goods ke liye nahi, aur ek article kehta hai ki taxable goods ki inter-state supply karne wale ko turnover se farak nahi padta, registration chahiye ([TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/goods-and-service-tax/gst-registration-exemption-upto-%E2%82%B940-lakhs-riders.html), [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/amp/story/news/exempt-small-suppliers-making-inter-state-supplies-on-e-commerce-platforms-from-gst-registration-gtri-news-301848)). Kyunki aap doosre states mein ship karoge, safe raasta ye hai ki **GST registration le lo** (ye mera conclusion hai, CA se confirm karo). Agar aap Amazon, Myntra ya Meesho jaise marketplace par bhi bechte ho, to pehli sale se registration zaroori hai ([ComplianceCalendar](https://www.compliancecalendar.in/learn/gst-registration-for-e-commerce-sellers)). Practical baat: payment gateways aksar GSTIN maangte hain ([TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/goods-and-service-tax/applicability-gst-online-business-services.html)). Bina registration ke penalty hoti hai: **Rs 10,000 ya tax ka 10%, jo zyada ho**, saath mein tax aur interest, aur maal detain ho sakta hai ([Masters India](https://www.mastersindia.co/blog/penalty-for-not-registering-or-late-registering-under-gst/)). Exact section ka reference sources mein alag-alag hai.

**GST rates (22 Sep 2025 se):** kapde aur footwear jinki price **Rs 2,500 per piece/pair tak** hai unpar **5%**, usse upar **18%** ([Bajaj Finserv](https://www.bajajfinserv.in/gst-on-clothes), [Taxscan](https://www.taxscan.in/top-stories/key-highlights-of-the-56th-gst-council-meeting-rate-rationalization-trade-facilitation-1432230)). Notes mein ye clear nahi hai ki Rs 2,500 tax ke bina hai ya tax ke saath; sirf forum opinion mila ([TaxTMI](https://www.taxtmi.com/forum/issue?id=120496)). Bags, belts, jewellery jaise accessories ke rates research mein nahi aaye, har item ka alag hota hai. HSN code: Rs 5 crore tak ke turnover par B2B invoice par 4 digit, usse upar 6 digit ([GST Council press release](https://gstcouncil.gov.in/sites/default/files/2024-02/press_release_hs_code_sac_issue.pdf)). Ek source (Taxmann) isse alag kehta hai, jo shayad galat hai. Rs 5 crore se neeche e-invoicing zaroori nahi ([Fonoa](https://fonoa.com/blog/india-lowers-e-invoicing-threshold-to-inr-50-million)). Returns ke due dates purane calendars se aaye hain, to GST portal se verify karo.

**Income tax aur Udyam.** Income-tax Act 2025 1 April 2026 se laagu hai aur purani presumptive schemes ab Section 58 mein hain: Rs 2 crore tak turnover par digital receipts ka 6% deemed profit ([TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/income-tax/analysis-presumptive-taxation-section-58-income-tax-act-2025.html)). Ye aapke case mein lagega ya nahi, CA batayega. Udyam registration optional hai, par trademark fee Rs 9,000 ki jagah Rs 4,500 per class ho jati hai ([Intepat](https://intepat.com/blog/trademark-registration-fees-india)). Notes mein business structure (proprietorship vs LLP vs Pvt Ltd), Shop and Establishment licence, trade licence aur professional tax par **koi sourced jaankari nahi mili**; ye sab CA se poochho. Marketplace TCS (0.5%) sirf marketplace sales par kata jata hai, apni website par nahi ([GSTGyaan](https://gstgyaan.com/summary-of-notifications-issued-us-52%281%29-of-cgst-act-2017)); iske rate par sources mein conflict hai.

## Payments aur shipping: sirf licensed raasta

**Payment gateway (licensed Payment Aggregator) use karo, personal UPI QR nahi.** RBI ki 2025 Master Direction ke hisaab se non-bank PA ko RBI authorisation chahiye aur merchant ka paisa bank escrow account mein rakhna padta hai ([RBI](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewMasDirections.aspx?id=12896)). Gateway merchant KYC karta hai; **Rs 40 lakh tak ke turnover wale merchant ke liye simplified KYC** hai (PAN, contact verification, proprietor ka ID) ([RBI](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewMasDirections.aspx?id=12896)). Card details apne server ya database mein kabhi store mat karo ([RBI](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewMasDirections.aspx?id=12896)); gateway ka hosted/redirect checkout lo, usse PCI ka kaam halka rehta hai. Settlement ka time contract se tay hota hai, RBI ne T+1 fix nahi kiya (kuch news sources abhi bhi T+1 likhte hain). Ek secondary source kehta hai ki existing merchants ki KYC ki deadline 15 Sept 2026 thi, par ye RBI text mein confirm nahi hua. Personal UPI par business payments lene se GST registration ka obligation to nahi badalta, par tax departments UPI data use karte hain; Karnataka mein ~14,000 unregistered businesses is data se pakde gaye ([Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/business/banking/is-upi-in-india-at-risk-cash-only-signs-return-as-some-shops-reject-e-payments-1.500212724)). Isliye WhatsApp orders ka paisa bhi business account ya gateway payment link se lo.

**Authentication aur refunds.** 1 April 2026 se domestic digital payments mein two-factor authentication zaroori hai, card-not-present mein har transaction ke liye unique factor; SMS OTP chalta hai ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/finance/news/rbi-two-factor-authentication-digital-payments-guidelines-2026-125092501154_1.html)). Ye bank/gateway handle karta hai, aapko bas gateway ka standard checkout use karna hai. Refund original payment method mein jana chahiye ([RBI](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewMasDirections.aspx?id=12896)). Failed transaction ke TAT aur Rs 100/day compensation 2019 ke RBI circular se aate hain ([TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/rbi/banks-pay-compensation-customer-failed-transactions.html)); UPI merchant failures ke T+5 jaise numbers sirf blog-level hain.

**COD, invoice, shipping.** Notes mein COD ko ban ya alag regulate karne wala koi India-specific kanoon nahi mila; COD ko normal sale ki tarah treat karo, GST invoice ke saath. COD fee checkout par pehle dikhao; us par GST treatment sourced nahi hai. Registered seller ko GST tax invoice dena hota hai (Rule 46 ke contents, jaise GSTIN, HSN, place of supply, notes mein sirf background knowledge hain). Price tax-inclusive MRP mein dikhao aur dual MRP mat rakho ([S.S. Rana](https://ssrana.in/corporate-laws/legal-metrology-and-packaging/)). Seller hi customer ke saamne responsible hai, courier ki galti par bhi; courier se claim aap karoge. Delivery ki koi fixed legal deadline ya "14 din refund" rule notes mein confirm nahi hua. E-way bill Rs 50,000 se upar ke consignment par lagta hai ([Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/business/e-way-bill-generation-without-b2b-e-invoice-details-to-be-blocked-from-march-1-2837978)), jo normal D2C parcel ke liye rarely relevant hai. Export karna ho to IEC chahiye ([DHL](https://www.dhl.com/discover/en-in/ship-with-dhl/export-with-dhl/required-export-documents)) aur LUT/FEMA ka alag setup; ye abhi scope se bahar rakho.

## Website par kya dikhana hai aur kya nahi karna

**Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules 2020** aap par lagte hain, kyunki own-stock seller "inventory e-commerce entity" hota hai. Website par prominently dikhao: legal name, headquarters/branch address, website details, customer care email aur phone, aur **grievance officer ka naam aur contact**; officer **48 ghante mein acknowledge** kare aur **ek mahine mein resolve** kare ([S.S. Rana](https://ssrana.in/articles/consumer-protection-e-commerce-amendment-rules-2026-recalibrating-e-commerce-compliance-across-the-indian-data-privacy-framework/), [IndiaLaw](https://www.indialaw.in/blog/civil/consumer-protection-e-commerce-rules/)). Saath mein: return/refund/exchange/warranty terms, delivery aur shipping charges, payment methods, cancellation procedure, country of origin, aur checkout par **total price ka breakup** (delivery, tax, other charges) ([IndiaLaw](https://www.indialaw.in/blog/civil/consumer-protection-e-commerce-rules/)). Cancellation charge tabhi lagao jab aap khud cancel karne par waisa hi charge bharte ho. Defective, galat ya late maal ka return/refund mana nahi kar sakte ([Mondaq](https://mondaq.com/india/dodd-frank-consumer-protection-act/985606/the-consumer-protection-e-commerce-rules-2020)). **Kanoon "change of mind" wali no-questions return nahi deta**; ye aapka business choice hai, par jo policy likho wo nibhao. Penalties Consumer Protection Act 2019 ke through aati hain.

**Dark patterns (13 types) pehle se banned hain** (Dark Patterns Guidelines, Nov 2023). Fashion site par risky cheezein: fake countdown timer, jhootha "only 2 left", pre-ticked gift wrap ya add-on, shipping fee sirf last step par dikhana (drip pricing), "No, mujhe discount nahi chahiye" jaisa confirm-shaming button, unsubscribe chhupana, aur undisclosed paid influencer posts ([Bar and Bench](https://www.barandbench.com/view-point/dark-patterns-in-india-from-hidden-practices-to-regulatory-spotlight)). Purchase ke liye consent explicit hona chahiye, pre-ticked box nahi ([TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/corporate-law/ccpa-mandates-e-commerce-self-audits-dark-patterns.html)). Enforcement abhi tak badi platforms par hua hai (Aug 2026 mein 9 platforms par Rs 1 se 7 lakh ke fines, jaise Zepto Rs 7 lakh) ([Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/corporate/ccpa-fines-9-digital-platforms-including-zepto-indigo-over-dark-patterns)). Chhote shop ke liye realistic risk pehle brand notices, Meta account suspension aur customer complaints hain, regulator fine baad mein; par duties sab par same hain.

**Misleading ads aur influencers.** Har ad (Instagram post bhi) truthful ho. CCPA penalty Rs 10 lakh tak, repeat par Rs 50 lakh tak, aur endorser ban ho sakta hai ([Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/news/story/influencers-take-note-centre-issues-new-social-media-regulations-with-fines-up-to-rs-50-lakh-366984-2023-01-21)). Notes ek aur conflict batate hain: kuch sources Rs 10/50 lakh ko CCPA ke Section 21 se jodte hain aur kuch Section 89 (court, jail) se; dono ko live risk maano. Influencer ko free product ya paise dene par clear disclosure ("#ad", "#collaboration") likhwao, sirf Instagram ka paid-partnership tag kaafi nahi maana jata ([GryNow](https://www.grynow.in/blog/asci-guidelines-for-influencer-marketing-in-india.html)). "100% pure silk/genuine leather" tabhi likho jab proof ho. Fake ya paid reviews mat likho, sirf negative reviews delete mat karo; BIS ka IS 19000:2022 abhi voluntary hai ([S.S. Rana](https://ssrana.in/articles/bis-standards-for-verifying-consumer-reviews-ensuring-transparency-integrity/)).

**Product labelling.** Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules ke hisaab se pre-packed garment par aur online product page par ye dikhna chahiye: maker/packer/importer ka naam-address, product ka naam, net quantity, MRP (sab taxes ke saath), country of origin ([S.S. Rana](https://ssrana.in/articles/compliances-for-website-e-commerce-website/), [Lakshmikumaran](https://lakshmisri.com/insights/articles/aiming-greater-transparency-on-sale-of-packaged-commodity-online)). Penalty first offence par Rs 25,000 tak, repeat par zyada aur jail tak ([S.S. Rana](https://ssrana.in/corporate-laws/legal-metrology-and-packaging/legal-metrology-notice-india/)); ek Amazon seller par Rs 1 lakh ka fine laga tha ([Greater Kashmir](https://m.greaterkashmir.com/article/lmd-penalises-amazon-seller-with-rs-1-lakh-penalty/425674)). Rule number par sources conflict karte hain (6(10) vs 4(10)). Normal fashion apparel ke liye BIS mark mandatory nahi bataya gaya, aur mandatory fibre/care labelling sirf proposal stage mein (Dec 2025 reports) ([Apparel Resources](https://apparelresources.com/business-news/policy/india-reportedly-planning-mandatory-labelling-apparel-home-textiles)); notified hui ya nahi, confirm nahi. Accessories (footwear, bags, jewellery, sunglasses) ke liye BIS compulsory registration list alag se check karo.

## Privacy, security aur WhatsApp: abhi kya, May 2027 tak kya

**Aaj ka kanoon aur kal ka.** Abhi (Oct 2026) IT Act ka Section 43A aur SPDI Rules 2011 laagu hain, DPDP ke main duties nahi. DPDP Rules Nov 2025 mein notify hue; notice, consent, security, breach reporting, erasure aur data-principal rights wale rules **~13/14 May 2027** se laagu honge, aur 43A tab hat jayega ([Shardul Amarchand](https://www.amsshardul.com/insight/enforcement-of-the-dpdp-act-and-notification-of-the-dpdp-rules/), [MirvoLegal](https://www.mirvolegal.com/insights/dpdp-rules-2025-compliance-timeline/)). Jan 2026 mein MeitY ne 18 mahine ko 12 mahine karne ka proposal diya tha (mainly bade players ke liye); 20 Sep 2026 tak koi amendment notify nahi hua tha, aur 20 Sep ke baad ka status confirm nahi ([MirvoLegal](https://www.mirvolegal.com/insights/dpdp-rules-2025-compliance-timeline/)). Salah: May 2027 ko target karo, par taiyari aise karo jaise date pehle aa sakti hai, aur meity.gov.in dekhte raho. **Chhote business ke liye koi blanket exemption nahi hai**; Section 17(3) sirf government ko exemption notify karne ki power deta hai ([KSK](https://ksandk.com/data-protection-and-data-privacy/dpdp-compliance-for-startups-msmes/)). Penalty ceiling Rs 250 crore tak report hui hai ([MirvoLegal](https://www.mirvolegal.com/insights/dpdp-rules-2025-compliance-timeline/)); ye ceiling hai, chhote shop par itni nahi lagegi, par category-wise mapping sources mein unclear hai.

**May 2027 ke baad ke duties (sources ke hisaab se):** alag, saral bhasha ka privacy notice jisme data aur purpose list ho; specific consent; reasonable security (encryption/masking, access control, logs, backups); breach hone par Data Protection Board ko turant bataana aur **72 ghante mein poori detail**, saath mein har affected customer ko batana; kaam khatam hone par data erase karna (logs kam se kam 1 saal rakhne hain); grievance **90 din** mein resolve; aur 18 se kam umar walon ke liye parent ki verifiable consent, unpar tracking ya targeted ads nahi ([Shardul Amarchand](https://www.amsshardul.com/insight/enforcement-of-the-dpdp-act-and-notification-of-the-dpdp-rules/), [MirvoLegal](https://www.mirvolegal.com/insights/dpdp-rules-2025-compliance-timeline/)). Cookies ka naam kanoon mein nahi hai, par practitioners kehte hain ki analytics aur Meta Pixel opt-in ke baad hi chalayein ([CookieChimp](https://cookiechimp.com/guides/regulations/in_dpdpa), vendor source). Ye vendor view hai, binding guidance nahi. Order deliver karne ke liye diya gaya address "legitimate use" maana ja sakta hai, par ye notes mein background knowledge hai, verified nahi.

**Security aur incident reporting.** Minimum: HTTPS, passwords hashed, admin/hosting/Google/Meta/WhatsApp accounts par MFA, backups, staff ko sirf zaroori access, card data kahin nahi. CERT-In ke 2022 Directions ke hisaab se cyber incident **6 ghante mein report** karna hai (incident@cert-in.org.in), logs 180 din rakhne hain; kuch sources kehte hain individuals/small enterprises excluded hain, to ye conflict hai ([Ankura](https://ankura.com/insights/everything-you-need-to-know-about-indias-new-guidelines-related-to-cyber-incident-reporting-by-cert-in)). Sensible approach: hosting platform ke logs use karo aur hack hone par report karne ka plan likh ke rakho.

**WhatsApp aur SMS marketing.** Customer ne khud message kiya ho aur aap one-to-one jawab do, to risk kam hai (ye notes ka inference hai). Bulk promotions par rules lagte hain. WhatsApp par marketing broadcast ke liye opt-in chahiye jisme business ka naam ho (third-party summaries; Meta ke official pages nahi dekhe gaye) ([Optimove](https://academy.optimove.com/hc/en-us/articles/25863306876829-Regulatory-Requirements-for-WhatsApp-Campaigns)). Commercial SMS ke liye DLT registration (sender aur templates) aur DND respect zaroori hai ([Exotel](https://developer.exotel.com/docs/faqs/trai-regulations)). Practical rules: marketing ka checkbox unticked ho, consent ka date/time/source save karo, STOP turant maano, kharide hue lists ko message mat karo, customers ko bina poochhe groups mein mat jodo. Order-related messages (confirmation, tracking) order se jude service messages hain.

**IP aur chhote jokhim.** Copyright (Section 63) aur Trade Marks (Section 103) mein 3 saal tak jail aur Rs 2 lakh tak fine ka provision hai, cognizable aur non-bailable bataya gaya, par minimum punishment par sources alag hain ([Nishith Desai](https://www.nishithdesai.com/research-and-articles/hotline/dispute-resolution-hotline/bombay-high-court-offences-under-the-trade-marks-act-and-copyright-act-are-non-bailable-4696)). Fashion mein practical enforcement mostly civil hai: tez injunction, social handles ka takedown, domain freeze ([LiveLaw](https://www.livelaw.in/amp/high-court/delhi-high-court/reliance-retail-ltd-yousta-fashion-brand-trademark-infringement-rogue-websites-social-media-accounts-fraud-284544)). Isliye: replica ya "first copy" mat becho, kisi brand ke photos bina likhit permission ke mat use karo, apni photos ke liye photographer se written assignment lo, models se written consent lo, fonts/music/stock images ka commercial licence lo, aur apna brand Class 25 mein file karo (Rs 4,500 per class individual/Udyam ke liye; 6 se 18 mahine lagte hain) ([Intepat](https://intepat.com/blog/trademark-registration-fees-india)). Giveaways mein entry fee ya "purchase karo aur lucky draw" mat rakho; closed-system gift vouchers (sirf aapke store par) RBI-regulated nahi hain ([RBI FAQ](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/FAQView.aspx?Id=126)). Accessibility (alt text, contrast) ke liye koi specific small-shop statute nahi, par Supreme Court ke 2025 ke order ki taraf trend hai ([LiveLaw](https://www.livelaw.in/amp/supreme-court/e-governance-platforms-digital-payment-systems-govt-websites-must-be-accessible-to-persons-with-disabilities-supreme-court-290853)); judgment ki poori scope ab verify nahi hui.

## Go-live checklist: must-have vs good-to-have

Ye checklist research ke findings se banayi gayi hai. "Must" ka matlab hai bina iske launch mat karo (ya CA se likhit confirm karke hi skip karo). Jahan "CA se confirm" likha hai, wahan notes mein source conflict ya gap hai.

| # | Kaam | Must / Good | Note |
|---|------|-------------|------|

| 4 | GST tax invoice har order par (sahi rate: Rs 2,500 tak 5%, upar 18%) | Must | Rs 2,500 tax-inclusive hai ya nahi, CA se confirm |

## Conclusion

Research ka sabse kaam ka nateeja ye hai ki aapka asli risk "bada kanoon tod dena" nahi, balki chhoti, mundane cheezein hain: GST registration ka gap, personal UPI se payments, footer mein seller details na hona, jhootha timer ya strikethrough price, aur bina permission ke photos ya music. Ye sab kuch hafton mein theek ho sakta hai aur kharcha kam hai. Badi cheezein (DPDP, E-Commerce Amendment, Legal Metrology 2026) calendar par hain, aur 1 Jan 2027 sabse pehle aati hai, to agar launch uske aas-paas hai to "good-to-have" items (30-din price record, dark-pattern audit) ko seedha "must" maano.

Dusri baat: notes ki quality ek limit hai. Zyadatar findings law-firm aur vendor blogs se hain, primary gazette nahi, aur kai jagah sources aapas mein takraate hain (GST registration, penalty sections, DPDP date, CERT-In scope). Isliye is report ko CA aur ek consumer/IT lawyer ke saath ek baar ka review session ka agenda samjho. Sabse zyada value wahan milegi: GST ka decision, final Privacy Policy aur T&C, aur 2026 amendments ka notified text.
